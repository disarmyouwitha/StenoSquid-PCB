# Design history

The [current KiCad project](../pcb/README.md) lives in one directory. Earlier layouts, review notes, artwork studies and tooling are available in Git at the snapshots below.

The `rev-d` tag remains the earlier electrical-review freeze. The owner-selected mechanical update on 2026-11-16 changes Rev D and its capacity options to the full-length no-ears shape with a 2 mm switch-side trim and recentered artwork. Header positions, signal tracks and component placements are preserved. [Fit record](plate-geometry.md)
